TRENDS

TRENDS is a gene regulatory network resource spanning human and mouse organ systems and developmental states at cell-type resolution. It contains 444 human and 257 mouse GRNs that connect transcription factors, regulatory elements and target genes using paired or biologically aligned gene-expression and chromatin-accessibility profiles.

Browse networks by species, organ system, data form and organ/tissue, inspect source metadata and cell-type coverage, and open the corresponding Figshare collection. Networks were generated with scPECA from bulk, unpaired single-cell and paired single-cell multiomic data.
